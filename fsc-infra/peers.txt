# FSC parties of the demo: one row per party that holds a group certificate.
#
# Read by pki/bootstrap-peer.sh, which generates the certificates from it;
# `bootstrap-peer.sh --list` prints it for pki/regenerate-internal-certs.sh
# and the Makefile. Fields are separated by "|"; spaces around a field are
# ignored. Lines starting with "#" are comments.
#
#   name           key of the party; also the file name of its group
#                  certificate: <dir>/pki/org/<name>.pem and <name>-key.pem
#   dir            directory under fsc-infra/ that holds <dir>/pki/
#   oin            OIN, the subject serialNumber of the group certificate
#   org            subject O of the group certificate
#   hosts          DNS SANs of the group certificate; the first is its CN
#   internal_ca    the internal CA's CN is "GBO-DEMO Internal CA (<internal_ca>)";
#                  "-" means the party has no internal certificates
#   internal_org   subject O of the internal CA and the internal certificate
#   internal_hosts DNS SANs of the internal certificate (CN <name>-internal)
#
